I'm a Master's student in **Artificial Intelligence & Innovation** at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu) (expected May 2027), with coursework in Machine Learning (PhD-level), Deep Learning, AI Engineering, Advanced NLP, Multimodal Machine Learning, Computational Data Science, and Data Structures & Algorithms.

Before CMU, I worked at **American Express**—first as an Analyst Intern, then Analyst, and finally **Senior Analyst**. I led the migration of large-scale production ML feature pipelines from IBM mainframe to a proprietary cloud platform for online credit-risk decisioning, sustaining 99.9% uptime through canary deployments. I built lineage-aware ingestion and validation workflows, used feature selection and clustering to compress feature space by 43%, and engineered PySpark/HiveQL validation frameworks that saved 2500+ QA hours per quarter while supporting feature pipelines with $15M/year impact.

I hold a **Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics & Communication Engineering** and **Master of Science in Physics** from [BITS Pilani, Hyderabad](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in). During my undergrad, I was an ML Research Intern at the [Computer Vision Lab, University of Ljubljana](https://www.fri.uni-lj.si/en) (remote), integrating CNN segmentation into a biometric eye-ID pipeline and cutting inference latency to 27%. I also interned at **Adobe** as a Software Development Intern, analyzing Kubernetes cluster vulnerabilities and benchmarking security tools.

My research interests include **multimodal machine learning**, **LLMs and VLMs**, **efficient fusion**, and **production ML systems**. I'm currently working on **fine-tuning multimodal web agents** (Mind2Web, VisualWebArena), **privacy-compliant LLM feature engineering for Zulip** (summarization, identity-masking, inference optimization), and **efficient multimodal fusion with bottleneck transformers** on MOSI/MOSEI.

Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:mramnath@andrew.cmu.edu), [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/mrunalini-ramnath), or [GitHub](https://github.com/mrunalinir). You can also download my [CV](/cv/).
