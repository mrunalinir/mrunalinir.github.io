## Overview

PRISM is a research project at **Carnegie Mellon University (CMU)** studying how information flows through hierarchical fusion modules in multimodal models.

## Highlights

- **Fusion Design:** Designed and validated a hierarchical fusion variant that outperforms a symmetric baseline by **+2.7% Acc2/F1**.
- **Asymmetry Insight:** Showed that asymmetric AV/VA fusion can match symmetric performance with **fewer parameters**, a key insight for production multimodal designs.
- **Efficiency Findings:** Discovered that scaling fusion capacity from **4 to 12 tokens** on **CMU-MOSEI/MOSI** hurts performance (**-6% correlation**), revealing architectural waste.
