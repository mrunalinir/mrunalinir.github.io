## Overview

ScleraNext is a collaboration with the **University of Ljubljana (UL)** focused on building a lightweight, real-time iris/retinal segmentation system for biometric identification.

## Highlights

- **Latency Reduction:** Replaced a cascaded 2-stage segmentation CNN with a **single compact model**, reducing inference latency by **73%** for mobile/IoT edge deployment.
- **Model Benchmarking:** Benchmarked **YOLO, U-Net, MobileNet, DenseNet** on accuracy/latency tradeoffs and selected an optimized U-Net–based model.

Biometric identification, CNN segmentation, edge deployment, University of Ljubljana.
